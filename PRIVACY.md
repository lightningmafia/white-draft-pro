# White Draft Pro Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 September 2026

White Draft Pro is a Mac word processor that runs on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics, and does not send your documents to a server.

## What stays on your Mac

- Documents you create, open, or export
- Comments attached to those documents
- Preferences such as theme, focus width, spelling, and whether you have seen the start screen

These are stored in files you choose and in standard macOS app preferences (UserDefaults).

## Permissions

White Draft Pro uses the App Sandbox. It reads and writes only files you pick in Open/Save panels, and it can print.

The app does not require network access.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at [github.com/lightningmafia/white-draft-pro](https://github.com/lightningmafia/white-draft-pro/issues).
