# White Draft Pro

A native macOS word processor. Works offline. Your files stay on your Mac.

**Mac App Store name:** White Draft Pro  
**Bundle ID:** `com.whitedraftpro.app`

## Support

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/lightningmafia/white-draft-pro/issues) for help with White Draft Pro.

Please include:

- macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac)
- White Draft Pro version (White Draft Pro → About White Draft Pro)
- What you were doing, and what happened

## Privacy

Read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

White Draft Pro does not create an account, does not include analytics, and does not send your documents to a server.

## Requirements

macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
