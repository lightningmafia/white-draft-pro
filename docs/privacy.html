White Draft Pro Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

White Draft Pro is a Mac word processor that runs on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics, does not include advertising, and does not send your documents to a server.

There are no third parties. No SDKs phone home.

What stays on your Mac

  • Documents you create, open, or export
  • Comments attached to those documents
  • Preferences such as theme, focus width, spelling, and whether you have seen the start screen

These are stored in files you choose and in standard macOS app preferences (UserDefaults).

Retention and deletion

You control the files. Delete a document in Finder to delete it. Uninstalling White Draft Pro removes the app and its preferences. There is no account to delete and no server copy to request.

Permissions

White Draft Pro uses the App Sandbox. It reads and writes only files you pick in Open/Save panels, and it can print.

The app does not require network access.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the updated policy will be posted at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/lightningmafia/white-draft-pro.
