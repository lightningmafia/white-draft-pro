White Draft Pro

A native Mac word processor. Your files stay on your Mac.

White Draft Pro runs on your computer. It does not create an account. It does not include analytics. It does not send your documents to a server.

Open a file. Write. Save. Print. Comments stay on the document. Preferences stay on the Mac.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Available on the Mac App Store.
