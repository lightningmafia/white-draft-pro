Description
White Draft Pro is a native Mac word processor. It runs on your computer. It does not create an account. It does not send your documents anywhere.

Open a file, write, save, print. Documents stay where you put them. Comments stay on the document. Preferences stay in standard macOS settings.

The editor is built for writing, not for a ribbon. Set a focus width. Switch theme. Use macOS spelling. The start screen can be skipped.

No Microsoft account. No telemetry. No network entitlement. The app is sandboxed and only reads or writes files you pick in Open and Save.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
